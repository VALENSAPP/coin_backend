Valens Account Deletion

Manage your privacy with confidence. You can permanently delete your Valens account and associated personal information directly from the app.

Request Account Deletion

Sign In

Open the Valens app and log in to your account.

Open Wallet

Navigate to the Wallet tab and open the menu drawer.

Settings

Select the Settings option.

Privacy Settings

Open the Privacy Settings section.

Delete Account

Tap the Delete Account option.

Confirm

Confirm your request to permanently delete your account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Profile Information

Name, profile photo, bio and related details.

Account Details

Login credentials and account information.

User Content

Posts, uploads and user-generated content.

Preferences

Application settings and saved preferences.

Personal Data

Other information associated with your account.

Data Retention Policy

Certain information may be retained temporarily when required for:

  • Legal and regulatory compliance
  • Dispute resolution
  • Enforcement of agreements and policies
  • Fraud prevention and security purposes

Any retained information is securely protected and permanently removed once it is no longer required.

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or privacy, please contact the Valens Support Team.
